# Ulalam / Cookly / Sangkaply

## Features

### Ulams
User can search ulams using the readily available ingredients. This feature comes from the first version
Users can make a variation or version of an ulam
Users can delete their own ulam but not other users' ulam
Users can edit their own ulam but not other users' ulam
Cook ulam shows the steps one by one
- Difficulty is automatically calculated using the number of ingredients, number of steps, and estimated cooking time entered by the owner
History of cooked ulam, day and for lunch, dinner, or breakfast. Show what you have cooked for the past days to not repeat ulams
Browse your and other users ulams in feed or home
Add ulam to your "Specialties", a collection of ulams that you can cook
Users can bookmark an ulam/recipe
- Users can plan meals ahead by days, Monday => Breakfast: , lunch: , dinner: , Tuesday
### Social
Users can follow other users
Users can like(heart icon) ulams
User profiles
Users able to interact witheach other in ulam comments, heart other's ulams, see others profiles, follow each other 
### Authentication
Login using google account and apple account, (google first)
Authentication / OAuth or JWT
### AI opportunities
AI Cooking Instructor
- AI Meal Planner 
- AI Recipe Recommendations or Search ulam with ai
